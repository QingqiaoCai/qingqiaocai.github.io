Research

My research experiences span wearable physiological sensing, biochemical sensing, biomedical hardware development, and brain–computer interfaces. Together, these projects have shaped my interest in building integrated bioelectronic systems that connect reliable sensing interfaces with real-world health monitoring and human–computer interaction.

Wearable Bioelectronics & Biosensing

Development of wearable sensing platforms integrating microneedle interfaces, electrochemical sensing, analog front-end electronics, power management, embedded systems, and wireless communication for continuous biochemical and physiological monitoring.

Wearable Electrophysiology

Design of low-noise wearable electrophysiological acquisition systems for EEG and EOG monitoring, including high-precision analog front ends, PCB design, embedded processing, Bluetooth communication, and real-time signal acquisition.

Brain–Computer Interfaces

Exploration of implantable and wearable approaches for acquiring and interfacing with neural and electrophysiological signals, including large-scale neural recording, electrode implantation strategies, and human–computer interaction systems.

Research Overview

Selected Projects

Wearable Microneedle Bioelectronic System for Continuous Health Monitoring

University of Notre Dame · Jul. 2026 – Present

Developing an integrated wearable microneedle bioelectronic platform for continuous electrochemical sensing and physiological monitoring.

My work includes polymer microneedle fabrication and wearable hardware system development. I am designing a two-layer bioelectronic PCB integrating two electrochemical sensing channels, four open-circuit potential measurement channels, analog signal-conditioning circuits, power management, and Bluetooth communication.

Keywords: Wearable bioelectronics · Microneedles · Electrochemical sensing · Analog front end · PCB design · Wireless systems

Wearable EEG–EOG Monitoring System for Human–Computer Interaction

Sun Yat-sen University · Jul. 2025 – Present

Developing a wearable electrophysiological sensing platform for simultaneous EEG and EOG acquisition, targeting human–computer interaction and long-term physiological monitoring.

I designed and iterated the core acquisition hardware using the ADS1299 and ESP32-S3, including the bio-signal acquisition front end, power-supply circuitry, four-layer PCB layout, and Bluetooth Low Energy communication. I also implemented real-time wireless data transmission and am developing a MATLAB-based signal-processing workflow.

Keywords: EEG · EOG · ADS1299 · ESP32-S3 · PCB design · BLE · Electrophysiology

Electrochemical Sweat Glucose Sensing System

Sun Yat-sen University · Oct. 2025 – Dec. 2025

Built and evaluated an enzyme-based electrochemical system for sweat glucose detection, integrating electrode fabrication, signal-conditioning hardware, Arduino-based acquisition, Bluetooth communication, and mobile visualization.

I led development of the signal-conditioning electronics, implementing current-to-voltage conversion and amplification circuits for low-level electrochemical currents. I also fabricated Prussian Blue-modified glucose oxidase electrodes and performed electrochemical characterization and concentration–current calibration.

Keywords: Electrochemical biosensing · Glucose sensing · Enzyme electrodes · Transimpedance amplifier · Bluetooth · Mobile monitoring

Implantable Brain–Computer Interface for Large-Scale Neural Recording

Sun Yat-sen University · Mar. 2025 – May 2025

Contributed to the development of an implantable brain–computer interface designed for large-scale neural recording through magnetically guided electrode implantation.

My work focused on device and structural fabrication, including PDMS-based guide channels and magnetic head components. I developed reproducible channel-fabrication methods and fabricated tent-shaped electrode scaffolds using micro/nano stereolithography 3D printing to evaluate structural feasibility for large-area neural applications.

Keywords: Brain–computer interface · Neural recording · PDMS · Microfabrication · Magnetically guided implantation · 3D printing
